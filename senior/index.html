Teachers' Day ❤️

Dear Senior,

You may simply be our teacher, but for me, you have always been someone I could learn from and look up to. The word "Senior" is what I call you — not just as a title, but as something that carries a lot of quiet respect and warmth.

From all the little things you have taught me to all the moments that became memories, I am genuinely grateful. You walked into our lives through HTML tags and CSS properties, and somehow left behind lessons that were never really about code at all.

You taught us coding — but somewhere along the way, your lessons became about much more than that. You taught us to try again when something does not work. To be patient while learning. To keep going even when things feel difficult. To not be afraid of making mistakes, because every error is just a step closer to the answer.

Maybe you will not remember every class or every conversation. Maybe some of those moments were just ordinary days for you. But I know I will remember many of those little moments — the explanations, the patience,and also the mokka jokes...😂 and the way you made complicated things feel approachable.

So on this Teachers' Day, I just wanted to say:

Thank you for teaching.
Thank you for helping.
Thank you for being soooooooooooooooooo.....kindd🫠.
And thank you for being the kind of teacher whose lessons stay with us long after the class is over.

Happy Teachers' Day, Senior. ❤️

With lots of respect and luv
Your junior

For every lesson.

For every correction.

For every "try again".

For every little thing you taught us.

Thank you, Senior. ❤️

Happy Teachers' Day!
